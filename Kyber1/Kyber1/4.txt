With the rapid development of intelligent transportation systems, vehicular networks are facing increasing security challenges.
Vehicles continuously exchange safety-related messages, including traffic conditions, accident warnings, and cooperative driving information.

Ensuring the confidentiality and integrity of these messages is critical, especially in the presence of potential quantum adversaries.
Post-quantum cryptography provides a promising solution by replacing traditional public-key primitives with quantum-resistant algorithms.

In this experiment, a lattice-based key encapsulation mechanism is used to securely establish a shared secret,
which is then applied to encrypt vehicular communication data using symmetric cryptography.
